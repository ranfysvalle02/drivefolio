How overdrives work

Powered by m-stash

Organize Drive links into stable, shareable collections.

1. Claim your namespace

Claim a public username such as /your-name. Every overdrive you create lives under it.

2. Add your Drive links

Create an overdrive, then add videos, decks, or PDFs you already share from Google Drive. Your files stay there.

3. Share when you are ready

Each overdrive gets a direct link such as /your-name/launch-film. Visitors can open it directly or browse everything under your username.
