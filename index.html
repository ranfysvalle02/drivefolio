How your reel works

Powered by m-stash

A simple route from a Drive link to a shareable reel.

1. Choose your reel address

Claim a simple address such as /your-name. It is the link you will share when your reel is ready.

2. Add your Drive links

Add videos, decks, or PDFs you already share from Google Drive. Your files stay there; the reel simply presents them beautifully.

3. Share when you are ready

Your reel has one clean link, so clients and collaborators always see the latest version. You stay in control of what is public.
